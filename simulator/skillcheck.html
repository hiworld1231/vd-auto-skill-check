Controls and perk notes
SPACE hits the needle. GREAT is white in the game (green in the old overlay); GOOD is black (blue in the old overlay). The practice pointer is neutral. A miss ends Frenzy and resets the perk streak. The top-left checkbox follows measured Frenzy transitions after the captured route ends, until you miss. Leaving starts the tier’s return window. During Frenzy, a successful hit keeps the ring and rotating needle visible until the next check begins, matching the recorded continuous transition through 360° and beyond. Normal interval controls ordinary checks; Frenzy interval controls the delay from a hit to the next Frenzy check. Finite captured routes keep their exact angles; infinite continuation applies a measured relative angle and destination-zone geometry to the current check. Flawless Execution models streak effects while repairing alone. Outcomes are simulated; replay timing and zones are estimates.
